## Design clarifications and deferred work

Overlay deltas have no document assignment field, so WP04 applies them to the
effective closure only; root and document queries delegate unchanged to the
base. A composite has no unique root/document namespace, so it exposes closure
queries only and rejects root/document scope rather than inventing a fake root.
Consumers needing a member's root or document query that member view directly.

SHA-256 is not subtractively composable. Effective fingerprints are lazy and
canonical; structural hashing streams sized canonical iterators, while logical
annotation stripping/deduplication and signatures retain canonical temporary
sets. The results equal independent full encoders, but this checkpoint does not
claim O(delta) fingerprint time or memory. Reusable posting/Merkle summaries
belong to WP05/WP10, and no hidden index was introduced here.

Real mmap close/fork behavior remains WP06; WP04 exercises the dependency
contract through instrumented mapped-view leases. Formal corpus conformance,
fuzzing, mutation/branch coverage, allocation-failure injection, and adversarial
anonymous bridge generation remain WP09. The inherited WP02 NCIT RDF-list/
individual ambiguity reported by WP03 is unchanged and strict mapping was not
weakened. Native parity and performance baselines remain WP08/WP10. The known
setuptools license-metadata warning remains WP12.

## Handoff

WP05, WP06, Exact-OM, reasoners, projectors, and evaluators may rely on:

- one runtime-checkable immutable `OntologyView` surface implemented by exact
  snapshot, overlay, and composite sibling identities;
- `apply_delta` and `compose_views` performing no parse/resolve/encode/copy or
  base-sized construction work for ordinary named edits and resident members;
- canonical closure iteration, exact membership, annotations/extensions,
  signatures, completeness, capabilities, origins, and stable semantic
  fingerprints on every view;
- explicit materialization as the only operation that creates an independent
  concrete snapshot arena, with compaction remaining a persistent overlay;
- stable anonymous identity across imports, composition order, nesting, and
  materialization, with ambiguity rejected rather than silently unified; and
- semantic cache keys based on structural/logical/signature fingerprints,
  while edit-chain and role/order digests remain separate provenance keys.

WP05 may now layer reusable immutable indexes over these iterators and deltas.
WP06 may encode/materialize the same contexts and implement real mapped-view
lifecycle hooks. Consumers should continue accepting `OntologyView` and use
`coerce_snapshot`; they must not downcast, serialize, or reparse to communicate
in process.
